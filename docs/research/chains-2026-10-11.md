# Franchise-wide prices and hours: 11 October 2026

The owner asked for as many gyms as possible, noting that what one
franchise publishes usually holds for all its gyms, and for opening hours
too. So this pass read each big chain's own pages and puts what the chain
publishes for every club on each of its branches
(`packages/osm/src/chains.ts`, applied wherever GymGO builds a gym from the
map: the bundled Australian, US and European gyms, country downloads, and
Search this area).

## Rules

- Only the chain's own site counts; read through a web search's copy (the
  sandbox can't open the sites directly).
- **Exact** where the chain publishes one price table for every club (McFit,
  Fitness24Seven). Where each club sets its own price, the chain's figure is
  shown as **"From"** (its lowest published price) or **"About"** (its
  recommended price), and the gym page says: "Prices the chain publishes for
  all its clubs. Each club sets its own, so this one may charge more."
  Sign-up then goes to the gym's own site, not the chain's terms page.
- A fact applies only in the country it was read for (Anytime Fitness
  Australia's price isn't put on a US club).
- **Hours:** "open 24/7 to members" only where the chain says every club is.
  It replaces the map's hours. Staffed and visitor hours stay unknown.
- Promotions aren't prices. Matching is on the map's brand tag or a name
  that starts with the chain's.
