# Day-pass and membership prices: research on 30 September 2026

The owner asked for a deep search for gym day-pass prices and the prices of
signing up. This is what was looked for, what was found, and what went into
the app.

## How it was done, and its limits

- Only a gym's **own website** counts as a source. Review sites, directories
  and aggregators (fitfit, Wheree, ClassPass, TripAdvisor…) were ignored, even
  where they quote a price.
- The research sandbox's network policy refused a direct connection to every
  gym website tried (dohertysgym.com, anytimefitness.com.au, jetts.com.au,
  goodlife.com.au, fitnessfirst.com.au, snapfitness.com, revofitness.com.au,
  planetfitness.com). So each gym's site was read through a web search
  restricted to that site's domain, which returns the search engine's copy of
  its pages. That copy can be older than the live page.
- A figure went into the app only when the copy stated it outright, for that
  club, on a page we could name. Those facts cite the page, say they were read
  through a search copy, and carry this date (`CHECKED.searchCopies` in
  `packages/melbourne-data/src/build.ts`).
- Chain-wide "from" prices ("memberships from $14.95 a week") were not
  attached to any club: a franchise club sets its own price.
- Introductory and promotional prices (first month cheaper, $5 joining
  offers, 7-day trials) were left out: they aren't what joining costs.

## To do it properly

Open each gym's price page directly (from a machine, or an environment whose
network policy allows the gyms' domains), confirm or correct the rows above,
and resolve the two disagreements. Beyond these Melbourne gyms, the ~1,350
Australian and the US and European map-only gyms have no prices at all; a
price for any of them needs the same treatment, one club at a time.
