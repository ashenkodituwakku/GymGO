# Prices, day passes, hours and photos: second pass, 11 October 2026

The owner asked for a deep search for gyms with little or no information
about day passes, memberships and photos, checking each is the right gym
and franchise; then for opening hours too. This pass covered the 23 curated
inner-Melbourne gyms (the ones with full gym pages). The ~3,000 map-only gyms
in Australia, the US and Europe weren't attempted: each needs the same
one-club-at-a-time treatment.

## How it was done, and its limits

- Same rules as the [30 September pass](prices-2026-09-30.md): only the
  gym's **own website** counts. Directories, review sites, Facebook and
  ClassPass were used only to spot doubts, never as the source of a fact.
- The sandbox couldn't open any gym website, Google or Google Maps directly
  (the network policy refuses them; the page fetcher couldn't resolve them).
  Every page was read through a web search's copy of it, restricted to the
  gym's own domain. A copy can be older than the live page.
- **Right gym, right franchise:** a price went in only when the page it came
  from is the branch's own (its address, or a page only that branch has), or
  the operator has just one club. Chain-wide "from" prices were left out.
- Promotions (first weeks free, intro offers) aren't treated as the price.
- Where a gym's own pages disagree, every such price is shown as
  disagreeing, with what each page said ("Check with the gym").
- Facts cite their page and carry `CHECKED.searchCopiesOctober`
  (`packages/melbourne-data/src/build.ts`).

## Photos

None added. No gym here has a freely licensed photo on Wikimedia Commons,
and photos on gym websites, Google or Maps belong to their owners: GymGO
doesn't have the right to show them. Every gym still says "No photo
supplied", and members can add their own. Getting real photos means asking
each gym for permission, or a Google Places key (which shows Google's photos
under Google's terms).

## To do it properly

Open each page directly (from a machine, or an environment allowed to reach
the gyms' sites), resolve the three disagreements and the RBT question, and
ask gyms for photos they're happy for GymGO to show.
